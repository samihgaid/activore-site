Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Activore ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit activore.app or use any of our apps, including The Daily Drip and Sound Meter.

1. Information we collect

On the website

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily submit it through our waitlist form. We use it to notify you when our apps launch.
  • Aggregate analytics — we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first service that does not use cookies, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking. We see anonymous, aggregate data only (e.g., country-level visits, page popularity).

Inside our apps

Each app has its own privacy disclosures inside the app and on its store listing. In general, our apps are designed to minimize data collection:

  • The Daily Drip uses your device's location (with your permission) to fetch local weather. Location data is processed in real time and not stored on our servers.
  • Sound Meter uses your device's microphone (with your permission) to measure ambient sound levels. Audio is processed locally on your device and is never recorded, transmitted, or stored.

2. How we use information

  • To deliver and improve our products
  • To communicate launch news to people who have opted in via the waitlist
  • To understand aggregate usage trends so we can prioritize improvements

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary cookies for security and DDoS protection — these do not identify you personally.

4. Third-party services

We use the following processors who handle data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, security, analytics, email routing
  • Formspree (or equivalent) — to receive waitlist email submissions
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — for app distribution and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat (in The Daily Drip) — for managing subscription state
  • Tomorrow.io (in The Daily Drip) — for weather data

5. Data retention

Email addresses submitted to our waitlist are retained until you ask us to delete them or until 24 months after our apps' general public launch, whichever comes first. To request deletion, email info@activore.app.

6. Children's privacy

Our products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email info@activore.app with any request and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS, TLS) for data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated via the website or email.

10. Contact

Questions or requests? Email info@activore.app.
